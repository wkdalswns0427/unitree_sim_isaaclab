--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DESIGN RATIONALE (why this works)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Pendulum-leg failure (one leg permanently in air, one permanently grounded)
is locked out by four reinforcing constraints:

  1. feet_air_time is EVENT-BASED — pays only on the air→contact transition.
     A leg that never lands earns zero.  (The _positive_biped variant pays
     min(left_in_mode_time, right_in_mode_time) during single-stance and
     allows the pendulum gait to saturate both terms at the threshold.)

  2. no_single_foot_park penalizes parking on one loaded foot longer than
     max_single_stance_time = 0.5 s — directly punishes the grounded-leg
     side of the pendulum.

  3. flight_phase penalizes frames where neither foot is loaded while the
     robot is commanded to move.  At higher cadences the policy can shortcut
     to a hop ("jump to next step") which the per-foot air-time term doesn't
     forbid.  Pairs with no_single_foot_park: together they require
     exactly-one-foot-loaded during locomotion.

  4. leg_mirror enforces (left_delta + right_delta)² ≈ 0 in the sagittal
     plane (hip-pitch / knee / ankle-pitch).  Forces hips/knees/ankles into
     mirror-image anti-phase motion.

Per-side swing_knee_flexion breaks the asymmetric-posture local optimum:
only the swinging leg collects, so a one-leg-pendulum policy captures half
the signal vs. a true alternating gait.  Target 0.5 rad ≈ 29° gives clear
toe-clearance without forcing a high-stepping march.

flat_orientation_l2 = -2.5 (raised from H1's -1.0) keeps the torso upright
against the larger H1-2 arm/torso inertia.  bad_orientation termination at
0.9 rad cuts episodes before the policy can farm lie-down survival reward.

Dropping the arms from the action subset (13-DOF) shrinks the policy output
and removes the wholebody-momentum coupling that previously produced wobbly
arm swings.  Arms now follow the gait passively, anchored by
joint_deviation_arms and the wholebody-floating actuator config.
